## 1. Executive summary

- No clearly new public CEPEJ-AI mention dated within 2026-05-13 to 2026-05-20 was identified in accessible search results.
- Public attention remains centered on three CEPEJ AI workstreams:
  1. the **2026-2027 CEPEJ cyberjustice and AI mandate**;
  2. the **December 2025 Guidelines on the use of generative AI for courts**;
  3. the **Resource Centre on Cyberjustice and AI / AIAB reporting function**.
- The strongest recent external reference is the EEEI article by **Priscilla Pisani** on the CEPEJ Working Group on Cyberjustice and Artificial Intelligence's first 2026-2027 meeting in Strasbourg on 26-27 March 2026.
- Legal/policy repositories continue to catalogue the CEPEJ instruments: the Universidad de los Andes public-sector AI guidelines database added a record for the CEPEJ generative-AI court guidelines on 2026-04-05, and JuLIA continues to catalogue the 2018 European Ethical Charter.
- Public LinkedIn results were accessible but mostly older or only indirectly related. They show professional discussion around CEPEJ-CYBERJUST, CEPEJ AIAB membership, and broader AI-in-judicial-systems safeguards.

## 7. Trends and interpretation

- **No fresh weekly spike detected:** Search results for the monitoring window returned relevant CEPEJ AI materials, but not newly dated items from 2026-05-13 to 2026-05-20.
- **CEPEJ's AI work is being reframed from principles to implementation:** The 2018 Ethical Charter remains the normative anchor, while 2025-2026 references emphasize operational tools: generative-AI guidelines, AIAB reports, Resource Centre monitoring, and the proposed Observatory on AI in Justice.
- **Human oversight is the recurring public message:** Across CEPEJ, DGPJ/Homepage Juridica, Uniandes, and LinkedIn-adjacent judicial AI discussions, the repeated themes are non-replacement of judges, supervision, transparency, data protection, and bias prevention.
- **Policy dissemination is multilingual and institutional:** Portuguese justice-sector sources and Latin American public-sector AI databases are carrying CEPEJ outputs beyond the Council of Europe domain.
- **Upcoming watch points:** The EEEI article identifies the CEPEJ Plenary in Sibenik on 18-19 June 2026, the WG-CYBERJUST meeting in Strasbourg on 22-23 October 2026, and the European Cyberjustice Network meeting on 2 December 2026 as future monitoring targets.
