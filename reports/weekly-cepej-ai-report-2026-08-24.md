- **Date:** WordPress `datePublished` **1 July 2026**; `modified` **13 August 2026** (English) / **13 August 2026** (French). The modification falls inside the **previous** reporting window (10–17 August) and was not recorded in that report. Original publication is July 2026. Logged here as catch-up.
- **Author/publisher:** European Expertise & Expert Institute (EEEI) Working Group on AI
- **Type:** Practitioner survey report (255 validated responses from 8 countries)
- **URLs:**
  - [Artificial Intelligence in judicial Expertise – First Report and First European Survey](https://experts-institute.eu/en/projects/wg-ai/ai-in-judicial-expertise-first-report/) (English)
  - [L’intelligence artificielle dans l’expertise judiciaire – Premier rapport](https://experts-institute.eu/projets/gt-ia/ia-expertise-judiciaire-premier-rapport/) (French)
- **Relevance:** Direct CEPEJ-AI reference. Aligns with CEPEJ’s own “Shadow AI” / individual-use theme in the 2025 generative-AI guidelines and in CEPEJ(2026)7.
- **Quoted snippet:** “43 per cent are not familiar with the AI Act or the CEPEJ guidelines.”

- **Assessment:** Strong **practitioner-gap** evidence: CEPEJ instruments exist, but a large minority of judicial experts do not know them. Not an in-window publication.
