## 7. Trends and interpretation

1. **Third consecutive quiet week for dated CEPEJ-AI mentions.** After mid-July’s EEEI and Rule of Law signals, the 20–27 July, 27 July–3 August, and 3–10 August windows produced no new date-verified direct references. That remains consistent with a midsummer lull rather than a decline in the underlying relevance of CEPEJ instruments.
2. **AI Act hard-law chatter continues without CEPEJ soft-law citations.** Enforcement and Article 50 transparency began at the start of the prior window; Annex III justice-related high-risk duties remain at December 2027. Public commentary reviewed this week still centres on Commission/AI Office enforcement and Omnibus deferrals. Older May 2026 pieces that *do* cite the Charter (People of Internet; Risk Publishing) resurfaced because of the calendar milestone, but they are not new publications—and some still describe 2 August 2026 as the Annex III justice start date, which later Omnibus coverage contradicts.
3. **Date hygiene remains essential.** HTTP `Last-Modified` again moved on LGSEE (10 Aug), Uniandes (9 Aug), LexEthica (4 Aug), Oxford Spain (3 Aug), Risk Publishing (5 Aug), CloseIT (5 Aug), and GamingTechLaw (9 Aug) without matching content `dateModified`/`datePublished` changes—so headers alone are insufficient to count a weekly mention. Search also continued to resurface standing CEPEJ PDFs and older LinkedIn posts as if current.
4. **Human-centred framing is stable in carry-over sources.** EEEI, Kunda, Nastić, Manos et al., Oxford Spain, and May AI Act commentary continue to treat CEPEJ instruments as the operational reference for non-substitution, oversight, transparency, and data sovereignty—especially when national rules or AI Act Annex III justice uses are mapped onto the Charter.
5. **Watchpoints for the next cycles.** Per EEEI’s WG-CYBERJUST reporting: next Cyberjust meeting **22–23 October 2026**; 6th European Cyberjustice Network meeting **2 December 2026**. Near-term monitoring should also watch for justice-sector guidance that bridges CEPEJ tools and AI Act Article 50 / deferred Annex III duties, and for any AI Office materials that actually cite CEPEJ (claims of joint “implementation guidance” remain unverified against primary CEPEJ sources).
