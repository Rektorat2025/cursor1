- **English rendering of that sentence:** If the 2020 instrument was conceived under the influence of the European Ethical Charter on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in Judicial Systems (CEPEJ, 2018) in order to regulate predictive and analytical tools that automate case flow, the new act faces the rise of generative artificial intelligence.
- **Assessment:** A public use of the Charter as historical influence on a revoked Brazilian resolution, followed by a transparency critique of the resolution that replaced it. ## 7. Trends and interpretation

1. **The week’s CEPEJ AI news is a meeting, not a document.** CEPEJ-GT-CYBERJUST met on 22–23 October, inside the window. The public record of that meeting is an observer note from UIHJ, not a CEPEJ adopted text and not a CEPEJ newsroom story that could be read. The note’s value is the work programme it confirms: automation, data sovereignty, deepfakes and electronic evidence, the Observatory’s data model, and AI in enforcement. All of those items are described as drafts or discussions.
2. **The next meeting is already implicated.** CEPEJ-GT-EFF meets on 27–28 October 2026, after this window. The UIHJ note says that group has already asked CEPEJ-GT-CYBERJUST to review the AI and cybersecurity sections of draft enforcement guidelines. A public text from that meeting, if one appears, is the first thing the next window should look for.
3. **The 2018 Charter is still the instrument Brazilian commentary cites.** Brochado and Soares, and Dias da Silva and Santos, both measure CNJ Resolution 615/2025 against the Charter and the EU AI Act. Neither cites CEPEJ(2025)18Final, which is the CEPEJ text written for generative AI in courts, nor CEPEJ(2026)7. The JOTA column uses the Charter as the ancestor of a resolution that has been revoked, then argues that the replacement makes disclosure of generative-AI assistance optional. That is a different transparency claim from paragraph 58 of the 2025 guidelines, and the column does not cite that paragraph.
4. **Paragraph 58 and paragraph 61 were not picked up.** No new text quotes the “no duty to justify departure” sentence or the documentation duty.
