This week is the same. What moved were a dual-language WordPress `modified` stamp (EEEI expertise, 2 September) and an **availability loss** (Juristech CEPEJ syntheses now 404). Diffusion of the *theme* remains easier than diffusion of the *CEPEJ name*.
